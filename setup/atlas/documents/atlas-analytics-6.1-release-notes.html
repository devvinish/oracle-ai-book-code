Atlas Analytics 6.1 Release Notes

Released

Atlas Analytics 6.1 was released on 1 July 2026.

New

Dashboards can be exported to Excel with Share > Data (Excel): every chart becomes one worksheet.

Scheduled reports now pause automatically after three failed deliveries in a row, and the schedule owner is notified. Resume them under Reports > Schedules after fixing the address.

A new date filter, Issue date, makes revenue in Analytics match the invoice lists of Atlas Billing.

Changed

Exports larger than 50 MB are sent by e-mail as a download link valid for 7 days.
