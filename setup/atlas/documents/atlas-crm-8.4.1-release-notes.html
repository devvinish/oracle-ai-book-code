Atlas CRM 8.4.1 Release Notes

Released

Atlas CRM 8.4.1 was released on 14 September 2026 for all accounts. No action is required: accounts are updated automatically.

Fixed

The pipeline board no longer stays on a loading spinner when deal cards show many custom fields.

Contacts merged with Find duplicates now keep the newest phone number instead of the oldest.

Forecast accuracy is calculated correctly for quarters that start in a month other than January.

Improved

The importer now shows the row number of every rejected row, and lets you download the rejected rows as a CSV file.

Exports of more than 100,000 records are prepared in the background and sent by e-mail.
